Campus Hazing Transparency Report

Reporting period: January 1, 2025 - December 31, 2025

Incident 1: Sigma Alpha Fraternity

In September 2025, new members of Sigma Alpha Fraternity were required to perform physically demanding tasks late at night as part of an unofficial initiation ritual. The organization was investigated and found responsible for hazing. No alcohol or drugs were involved. Sanction: probation through Fall 2026.

Incident 2: Women's Club Rowing

In October 2025, members of the Women's Club Rowing team required new members to consume alcohol at a team event. The organization was found responsible for hazing. Sanction: loss of club-sport funding for one year.
